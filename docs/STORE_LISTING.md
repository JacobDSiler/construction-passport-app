## Full description
Keep your Safe Pass, manual handling cert and every other ticket in one place, on your phone, with expiry dates. Show your passport to a contractor with a QR code; no app needed on their end.

Free forever: your Passport and sharing. After a 90-day free trial, the working tools (timesheets, job sheets, diary, audits, expenses) are 3.80 EUR a month, or free if your employer has a company plan.

Built for the Irish construction industry. Every screen can show a second language beneath the English: Romanian, Polish, Lithuanian, Ukrainian, Russian, Portuguese, French, Italian, Spanish, Irish and Filipino.

Provided by Construction Passport (Prime Management 18).

## Google Play data safety (answers)
- Data collected: name, email, phone, photos, approximate/precise location (timesheet, optional), user content (expenses, reports).
- Shared with third parties: none sold. Hosting provider processes data on our behalf.
- Encrypted in transit: yes. Deletion request: yes, in the app and by email.
- Ads: none. Target audience: 18+.

## Apple App Privacy
Data linked to user: contact info, user content, photos, location (optional). Not used for tracking. No third-party ads.
