Construction Passport: Privacy Policy

Draft for review by Prime Peak Limited before publication. Last updated 28 November 2026.

Who we are

Construction Passport is provided by Prime Peak Limited, trading as Prime Management 18, Unit 13 Meag Mullingar Enterprise Centre, Mullingar, N91 Y099, Ireland. We are the controller of the personal data described here. Contact: primepeak@primemanagement18.com.

What we collect

Only what you add to the app:

  • Your name, trade, phone number and employer.
  • Your qualifications and certificates, including photos you take or choose, and expiry dates.
  • If you create an account: your email address and password (the password is stored by our sign-in provider, not readable by us).
  • Timesheet entries (start, end, breaks, notes) and, if you allow it, the location at the time of an entry.
  • Expenses and receipt photos, hazard reports, site diary and audit entries, job sheets.
  • A passport code and an owner token that let the app find your passport.

The wellbeing check-in stays on your phone and is not sent to us.

Why we use it

To run the app: to show your qualifications to people you choose to share them with, to back up your passport, and to let your employer see timesheets and expenses you send them. We do not sell your data and do not use it for advertising.

Who can see it

Your shared passport (name, trade, employer, qualifications and certificate photos) is visible to anyone you give the link or QR code to. Your employer sees timesheets and expenses once you join their crew. Certifiers you or your employer deal with can verify your qualifications. Nobody else can see your data.

Where it is stored

Data is stored with our hosting provider, Supabase. Hosting region: [to confirm before publishing]. Payments for company plans are handled by Stripe; we do not see card numbers.

How long we keep it

Until you delete it or delete your account. You can delete your data inside the app.

Your rights

Under GDPR you can ask to see, correct, export or delete your data, object to its use, or complain to the Data Protection Commission (dataprotection.ie). Write to the email above.

Children

The app is for working adults and is not directed at children.

Changes

We will post changes here and update the date above.
